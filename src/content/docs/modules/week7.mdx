For your midterm you will present the brand you have been building since Week 1, as a single document that pulls all your work together.

Everything you need is already made. Over the past six weeks you have produced a banner campaign, a logo, a colour palette and a set of icons. This assignment asks you to present them as one brand, and to explain the thinking behind your decisions.

**Your presentation must contain all four of these:**
1. **Logo** (Your final mark, in light mode and dark mode)

2. **Colour palette** (Your swatches with hex codes, and the harmony you used)

3. **Icons** (Your full icon set, shown together)

4. **Banners** (All three formats from Exercise 1 — horizontal, square, vertical)

Written explanation (Your thinking behind the brand (see below)).

## How to structure it

1. **Cover** — your brand name and logo
2. **About the brand** — what it is, who it is for, what it sells
3. **Logo** — light mode and dark mode versions, shown on their backgrounds
4. **Colour palette** — swatches with hex codes, and the harmony you used or the photo you pulled them from
5. **Icons** — your set shown together, at a consistent size
7. **Banners** — all three formats
8. **Your explanation** — the written section

You can build this in Illustrator, Photoshop, Figma, Google Slides, Canva or anything else you are comfortable with. The tool does not matter. What matters is that it reads as one brand and is easy to look at.

Keep the presentation itself clean. White or very light backgrounds, generous space around your work, and nothing competing with the assets you are showing.

## Your written explanation

Answer these in 300–500 words. Write in your own words — I want to know what you were thinking, not what sounds impressive.

**The brand:**

What is your brand and what does it sell?
Who is it for?

**Your logo:**

What does the mark represent?
Why did you keep it simple the way you did?
Why does the dark mode version differ from the light one?
