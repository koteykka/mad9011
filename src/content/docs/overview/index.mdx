## Course Instructor - Kateryna Parashchuk

**Contacting Me:**

The best way to reach me outside of class is by email, or by Slack if you're already on the course workspace. Either is fine — use whichever is easier for you.

I reply during working hours, Monday to Friday. In most cases you'll hear back the same day or the next, and always within three business days. If you haven't heard from me after three business days, please send a follow-up. It means the message got lost.

A few things that help me help you faster:

Include a short description in the subject line
Message from your Algonquin email so I can identify you
If it's about an assignment, tell me what you've already tried and attach a screenshot if something isn't working

Please don't leave a question about an assignment until the night before it's due. I'm happy to help, but I can't guarantee a reply outside working hours.

If your question is quick, ask me in class or right after. That's usually the fastest answer you'll get.

## Invite to Our Slack Channel

<Icon name="slack" />
Slack is a communication tool used by developers all over the world to stay in touch with their development team. If you are familiar with Discord then you will recognize Slack. They have a very similar
layout. Slack is more geared towards communication for developers in a primarily text environment. Discord adds audio and video but we use MS Teams and Zoom for that part.

We will be using Slack to keep all our Faculty, Students, and Program Assistants in communication.

If you have already accepted the invite to the Slack Workspace but do not see a channel for any specific course, you can browse for the channels by going to the left hand menu and clicking on `Channels` > `Manage` > `Browse channels`.
