## Course Instructor - Kateryna Parashchuk

**Contacting Me:**

For anything related to exercises, assignments, or grades, please email me. It keeps everything in one place so I can track it properly and follow up. Slack is fine for quick, general questions if you're on the course workspace, but assignment questions should always come by email.

I reply during working hours, Monday to Friday. In most cases you'll hear back the same day or the next, and always within three business days. If you haven't heard from me after three business days, please send a follow-up. It means the message got lost.

A few things that help me help you faster:

Include a short description in the subject line
Email from your Algonquin address so I can identify you
If it's about an assignment, tell me what you've already tried and attach a screenshot if something isn't working

Please don't leave a question about an assignment until the night before it's due. I'm happy to help, but I can't guarantee a reply outside working hours.
