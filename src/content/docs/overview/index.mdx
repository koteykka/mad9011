## Invite to Our Slack Channel

<Icon name="slack" />
Slack is a communication tool used by developers all over the world to stay in touch with their development team. If you are familiar with Discord then you will recognize Slack. They have a very similar
layout. Slack is more geared towards communication for developers in a primarily text environment. Discord adds audio and video but we use MS Teams and Zoom for that part.

We will be using Slack to keep all our Faculty, Students, and Program Assistants in communication.

If you have already accepted the invite to the Slack Workspace but do not see a channel for any specific course, you can browse for the channels by going to the left hand menu and clicking on `Channels` > `Manage` > `Browse channels`.
